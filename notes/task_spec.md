# Mapping radar backscatter on Bodmin Moor 

How does Sentinel-1 radar backscatter vary over time, and where on Bodmin Moor does it show the clearest increase or decrease? Use public Sentinel-1 synthetic aperture radar (SAR) data to investigate a peatland area of your choice on Bodmin Moor, Cornwall. 

1. Produce a time series. Use observations from January 2023 to December 2024 and one polarisation (VV or VH). Select a representative patch within your study area and plot its backscatter against time. Show the observations or temporal summaries used to estimate the trend, and label the axes with units. 
2. Map the trend. Estimate the direction and magnitude of change across your study area over the same period. For example, map the slope in decibels per year for each grid cell. Mark the patch used for your time series and include a colour scale with units. 
3. Interpret the result. Describe the main pattern, including a weak or negligible trend if that is what you find. Discuss one factor that could influence the apparent trend and identify one field observation that would help you interpret it in terms of peatland condition.
