Using Sentinel‑1 synthetic aperture radar (SAR) data, please produce and plot backscatter time series for selected peatland locations on Bodmin Moor, Cornwall, over a period of your choice. Please show the trend at each location on a map of Bodmin Moor.

The purpose of the exercise is to understand how you investigate a question and interpret what you find. A few hours of focused work is sufficient, and preliminary results are welcome. If a question remains open, a brief note on how you would follow it up would give us something useful to discuss.

Once we receive your work, we will schedule an interview to discuss your approach and findings. We look forward to hearing your ideas.
